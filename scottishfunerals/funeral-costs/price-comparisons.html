To help us do this, we regularly compare our prices with the other major funeral service providers in the area. We hope this comparison will assure you of the high value of the services we provide. If you have any questions about our prices, please either visit the other pages in the Funeral Costs section of this website, send us a message, or call us on 0800 121 8090.

All members of the industry are professionally obliged to provide a basic and economic cremation option.

This includes in all cases:

  1. The arrangement of a basic funeral.
  2. Bringing the deceased into the funeral parlour .
  3. The provision of a basic coffin.
  4. The provision of a hearse only from the funeral parlour to the crematorium.
  5. Funeral director and four bearers.

Within the basic funeral packages offered by Fosters, Dignity and Co-op, all three providers offer these services, with the following variations:

 

Arrangement of the Funeral

This involves arranging a crematorium time slot, help and advice in organisation of the event, provision of a hearse to deliver the deceased directly to the crematorium, the services of a funeral director throughout the process.

There are variations between the providers in cost and choice of crematorium, as well as time and day of funeral :
