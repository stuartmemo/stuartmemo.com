Cost Calculator

Select amongst the many options available for a funeral and easily calculate and adjust costs

Welcome to our funeral cost calculator. This is the first of its kind in Scotland and has been designed to inform you of your choices with the added benefit of an instant, on-line quotation. Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions, or telephone 0800 121 8090.

How to use

Each Funeral Package has a basic price and a selection of optional extras which you may choose to add. To see what is included and what is optional for each package, just click the package button to see the options below.

Once you have selected the package and any options you wish, just send or print the details using the buttons at the bottom.
