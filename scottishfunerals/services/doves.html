Doves

Symbols of peace, of love, and of the passing of a loved one

For centuries doves have symbolised peace, tranquillity and love. A single white dove symbolises the spirit and soul of a loved one whose life has now ended. Releasing a dove can be a very personal and loving gesture for the bereaved.

An individual, or indeed the entire family may release doves. This can take place at the graveside or at the conclusion of the service at the crematorium. The doves can be released by the handler or given to a family member for them to release personally.

Total cost for one single dove £75. Each additional dove thereafter £50.

For more details please contact: 0141 647 8080
