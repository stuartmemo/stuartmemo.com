  • User adoption program

    Pick Protection commits to working in partnership with your organisation to maximise user adoption through our User Adoption Program.

  • Training

    High quality training is key to successfully implementing a lone worker solution. Our training is available in a range of options to suit your organisations requirements.

  • Communication

    Nobody goes to work expecting an accident or incident. Communication is key to encourage user adoption. At Pick Protection using our app we commit to regular communication with your lone workers.

  • Ongoing management

    Once a solution has been implemented it must be well managed to ensure continued high usage. Pick Protection works with your managers giving them the tools to efficiently manage their lone workers.
