Training + Support

At Pick Protection we believe you should be completely satisfied with your chosen lone worker solution, and confident that your workforce know how to use and implement your chosen solution in the event of an incident.

As a result, our services do not stop at lone worker solutions. We can also provide lone worker training for your workforce and management team to ensure that risk is reduced across the board. Thanks to our innovative lone worker security products, your workforce can rest assured that no matter the assignment, their safety is at the forefront of your mind.

Our training includes but is not limited to:

  • Webinars
  • Toolbox Talks
  • E-learning
  • Ongoing Organisation Support
