# Factory sample sources

All factory samples are compact derivatives of openly redistributable recordings. Each kit directory includes the upstream license and a file-by-file provenance table. Exact source and output checksums also live in `manifest.json`.

## Fischer 808

- Upstream: [Roland TR-808 Rhythm Composer Sound Sample Set 1.0.0](https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer)
- Creator: Michael Fischer / Technopolis
- Revision: `85fbecf1bec32553395625ea659e2a56dfd7c0e1`
- Rights: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Selection: Direct 16-bit recordings of a real TR-808, selected from Michael Fischer's 116-sample capture.

## Uzu Modern

- Upstream: [uzu-drumkit](https://github.com/tidalcycles/uzu-drumkit)
- Creator: Mot4i, Switch Angel, and Ludens
- Revision: `2f3e05c70ab4d73ad053a1467adec89bd27377a0`
- Rights: The Unlicense / public-domain dedication
- Selection: Synthesized and analog-processed electronic drums, including modular sound-design hits.

## Big Rusty Room

- Upstream: [Big Rusty Drums 1.100](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums)
- Creator: Karoryfer Samples
- Revision: `f07ce00df34a46b6b08375be56fe116cf15782bc`
- Rights: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Selection: Room-forward hits from a large vintage Polish acoustic kit, with four oversized toms and unusual cymbals.

## Swirly Brushes

- Upstream: [Swirly Drums 1.100](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.swirly-drums)
